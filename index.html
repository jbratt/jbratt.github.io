AI & Automation

Agentic Solutions

We help organizations move past pilots and put trusted, production-ready AI agents to work across sales, service and operations, with secure designs, sound data and measurable results.

AI Strategy & Readiness

Start with a plan you can act on. We work with your stakeholders to find and rank the AI use cases with the most business value. We then check how ready your data, platform and security are, and define clear success measures. You leave with a phased roadmap that takes you from a first pilot to full production.

Agentforce Design & Build

We design and build agents that act reliably, not just answer questions. Our team creates the Flows, Apex, prompt templates and integrations that let your agents complete real work, with smooth handoffs to your people when a task needs a human.

Data & Grounding

Give your agents accurate, trusted answers by grounding them in your business data. We design Data 360 data streams, data models and identity resolution to build unified customer profiles. We also prepare unstructured content such as knowledge articles, policies and documents with search indexes and retrievers.

Enterprise Integration

Connect agents to outside systems through MuleSoft, APIs and MCP clients. We also build custom MCP servers on Salesforce that make your CRM data, Flows and Apex logic available as secure tools for Agentforce and other AI agents and assistants.

Channels & Surfaces

Put agents wherever your customers and employees already work, including web and in-app messaging, Slack, voice, the Salesforce console and custom front ends through the Agent API. Handoffs to human teams are built in.

Security & Trust

Deploy AI with confidence. We design least-privilege access so each agent can reach only the data and actions it needs. We also set up the Einstein Trust Layer for data masking, audit trails and zero data retention. Guardrails keep sensitive information safe and help you stay compliant with your industry's regulations.

Connect Your Agents

Get more from the agents you already have by building on the Headless 360 platform. We connect them to the data, metadata and context they need from internal and external sources, so they give better answers and can take on more work.

Optimization & Managed Services

Tune performance, manage costs and expand your digital workforce over time.
