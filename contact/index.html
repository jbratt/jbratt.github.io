For more information about our services, or to launch a project with Practical Algorithm, please fill out this form or contact us via email or phone.

Practical Algorithm helps organizations put AI agents into production on the Salesforce platform. We design, build and run Agentforce solutions from start to finish. That starts with readiness assessments and use-case selection, then moves to agent architecture: subagents, actions, deterministic logic and human handoffs. Our team builds the Flows, Apex, prompt templates and integrations behind each agent. We also do the data work that makes agents useful, from grounding agents in Data 360 and knowledge content to connecting outside systems through MuleSoft, APIs and MCP.

Trust is part of every engagement. We set up least-privilege security models, configure the Einstein Trust Layer and guardrails, and build testing and observability practices so agents behave predictably at scale. After launch we stay on to tune performance, manage consumption costs and grow our clients’ digital workforce, so their AI investment turns into measurable business results.
